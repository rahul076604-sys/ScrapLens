🗂️ Waste Groups

Your 11 classes can also be understood through broader waste-management groups.

♻️ Recyclable

Materials that can commonly enter recycling systems when accepted by local facilities.

Glass • Cardboard • Metal • Paper • Plastic

🔄 Reusable

Items that may be used again, donated, repaired, or repurposed.

Clothes • Shoes

🌱 Organic

Biodegradable material that may be suitable for composting or organic waste collection.

Biological

⚠️ Special Handling

Certain materials require specific handling instead of normal household disposal.

Battery

🗑️ General Waste

Waste that does not fit the recyclable, reusable, or organic categories.

Trash

✨ Non-Waste

Items identified as not being waste and potentially still useful.

Non-Waste

🌱 ScrapLens Insight

Correct classification is the first step toward better waste management. Once waste is identified, it can be directed toward recycling, reuse, composting, special handling, or appropriate general disposal.
