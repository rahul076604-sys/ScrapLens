Build Better Waste Habits

Simple everyday choices can help reduce waste and protect our environment.

♻️ Reduce

Avoid unnecessary packaging and choose products that create less waste.

🔄 Reuse

Reuse bags, containers, clothes and other useful items instead of throwing them away.

🗂️ Recycle

Separate recyclable materials such as paper, plastic, glass and metal.

🌱 Compost

Where suitable, compost biodegradable food and garden waste instead of sending it to landfill.

🚯 Avoid Littering

Always dispose of waste responsibly and use appropriate bins or collection points.

💧 Save Resources

Save water and energy, and make thoughtful choices about the products you consume.
